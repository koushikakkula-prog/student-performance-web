How It Works

01
Enter Student Details

03

Trained Random Forest, Decision Tree, and Regression models evaluate holistic academic patterns.

04
Performance Prediction

Evaluation Standards

Performance Categories & Classification

Standardized grading thresholds used by the system for academic assessment.

90% – 100% Grade O
Outstanding

Exceptional mastery of subject matter with consistent stellar attendance and study habits.

80% – 89% Grade A+
Excellent

High-tier academic performance with strong exam scores and consistent coursework completion.

70% – 79% Grade A
Good

Solid performance across all subjects. Moderate increase in study hours will push into top tier.

60% – 69% Grade B
Average

Meets baseline requirements. Needs focused preparation in internal tests to avoid slipping.

40% – 59% Grade C
Needs Improvement

Moderate internal exam scores or low attendance. Requires focused faculty guidance and structured revision.

Below 40% Fail
Fail

Scores below passing threshold (40%). Identifies students in urgent need of academic counseling and remedial support.
